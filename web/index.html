WHY LILT

You think three times faster than you type. But voice typing has always made you talk like a robot — perfect sentences, no pauses, no second thoughts. Lilt listens the way a friend does. It hears what you meant, not just what you said.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps.
Zero effort.

  1. 01

    Tap the pill

    Or hold your hotkey. Lilt starts listening without moving your cursor or switching apps.

  2. 02

    Just talk — mistakes welcome

    Say “um”. Change your mind. Lilt quietly removes the filler and keeps only what you meant.

  3. 03

    Clean text appears

    Punctuated, capitalised and typed right where your cursor is. Hit send.

QUESTIONS

Good
questions.

Is Lilt really free?

Yes. Lilt is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence. No subscription, no ads, no account.

Which languages does it understand?

English with Indian accents is the focus, along with mixed Telugu-English (Tenglish) and Hindi-English (Hinglish) phrases like “kaadu kaadu” and “nahi nahi”.

Where does my voice go?

Audio is sent only to the speech engine you choose — your own API key (for example Groq) or a local Whisper model on desktop. Your history is stored on your device.

Why does the Android app need special permissions?

To float the pill over other apps it needs “display over other apps”. Microphone is for listening, and alarms need permission to show on the lock screen. Nothing else.

What does “silent alarm” mean?

The reminder still takes over your screen and stays until you tap Done or Snooze — it just doesn’t ring. Perfect for meetings, libraries and late nights.

Can I contribute?

Please do. Check CONTRIBUTING.md on GitHub — bug reports, new language rules and UI polish are all welcome.
